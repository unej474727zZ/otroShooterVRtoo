<h2>🚀 Overview</h2>
<p><strong>VR CQB Tactical Shooter</strong> is an advanced, high-performance VR Mobile Starter Kit for Android. Designed specifically for Google Cardboard and mobile VR headsets, it solves the most critical challenge in mobile VR: <strong>seamless Bluetooth gamepad integration with zero input latency.</strong></p>

<p>Combining the lightweight speed of Three.js / WebXR with native Android Java performance, this starter kit delivers immersive Close-Quarters Battle (CQB) tactical shooting at smooth, battery-friendly 60 FPS.</p>

<h2>⭐ Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>🎮 Native Bluetooth Gamepad Bridge:</strong> Direct Java-to-JS bridge supporting X3, Ipega, Xbox, and all standard Bluetooth controllers with analog stick smooth rotation and trigger fire.</li>
  <li><strong>📡 5-Point Tactical Proximity Radar:</strong> Diegetic, borderless VR radar. Displays player orientation in blue and tracks up to 4 closest active threats in red without HUD clutter.</li>
  <li><strong>🤖 Dynamic Enemy AI:</strong> Wave-based enemies with Hunter and Patrol behaviors. Features a 750ms reaction grace window with weapon sound cues to prevent unfair deaths.</li>
  <li><strong>🎯 Dual Hitbox System:</strong> Independent headshot (3x critical damage) and torso hitboxes with physical raycast projectile feedback.</li>
  <li><strong>🔊 Spatial 3D Audio & Night Vision:</strong> Positional audio cues, footstep warnings, and glowing dual-LED night vision goggles on enemy helmets in pitch darkness.</li>
  <li><strong>⚡ 60 FPS Mobile Optimization:</strong> Compressed WebP textures, low-draw-call GLB assets, and shader pre-warming to eliminate VR stuttering on Android.</li>
  <li><strong>📖 Comprehensive Documentation:</strong> Step-by-step HTML guide on how to build, reskin, modify weapon ammo, add levels, and change package IDs in Android Studio.</li>
</ul>

<h2>📦 What's Included in the Package</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Full Android Studio Source Code:</strong> Clean, unbloated project ready to import.</li>
  <li><strong>Ready-to-Test Demo APK:</strong> Pre-compiled APK to test immediately on your phone.</li>
  <li><strong>Offline HTML Documentation:</strong> Detailed customization guide with diagrams and code snippets.</li>
  <li><strong>All 3D Low Poly Assets:</strong> M4A1 rifle, industrial killhouse building, and animated soldier rigs.</li>
</ul>

<h2>⚙️ Technical Requirements</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Android Studio Hedgehog / Iguana / Ladybug or newer.</li>
  <li>Android SDK 24+ (Android 7.0 Nougat to Android 14+).</li>
  <li>Smartphone with Gyroscope & Bluetooth gamepad (Optional: VR Cardboard headset).</li>
</ul>
